JU OUTCASTS - PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. We are a student initiative, not a data-mining corporation! Here is how we handle your information:

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
When you register via our "Join Us" page, we collect your Name, WhatsApp Number, Department, and Course Year.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
Your information is used strictly for two purposes:
- To verify that you are a genuine student of Jadavpur University.
- To add you to our official WhatsApp group and maintain our member directory.

3. DATA SHARING
We will NEVER sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, university officials, or marketing agencies. Your data remains strictly with the JU Outcasts admin team.

4. DATA DELETION
If you decide to leave the community and want your details removed from our database/website, simply contact an admin or email us, and we will wipe your data immediately.

By joining, you consent to this basic collection of information to keep our community safe and verified.
